5. My recommendation: use three levels of planning
For your project, I would distinguish three levels:
1. Project level: Agree on the overall architecture, repository structure, technical tooling and major module boundaries.
2. Increment level: Agree on the next small capability, its interfaces, acceptance criteria and tests.
3. Code level: Let Codex make ordinary implementation decisions within those boundaries, review the changes, and verify them.
